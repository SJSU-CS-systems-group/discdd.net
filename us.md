# Who we are

This project is part the Reed Systems Group at San Jose State University, where we build systems for the benefit of humanity.

More than [two dozen undergraduate and graduate students](https://github.com/SJSU-CS-systems-group/DDD/graphs/contributors) have worked with [Ben Reed](https://www.sjsu.edu/people/ben.reed/), Associate Professor in Computer Engineering, to develop these applications.
We are collaborating with the [Department of Computer Science and Software Engineering](http://ipi.kpi.ua/) of Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute.
